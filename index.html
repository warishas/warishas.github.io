About

Design is a decision-making discipline.

I'm Warisha — a product designer with roots in mission-driven organizations and enterprise-scale products. I design at the intersection of AI, compliance, and human complexity — from surgical operating rooms to federal telecommunications mandates.

I hold an MS in Human Centered Design & Engineering from the University of Washington, where I led the design of a patent-pending AI tool recognized for innovation. Good design earns trust through decisions that are clearly reasoned and rigorously tested.
